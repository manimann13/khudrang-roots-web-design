# Khudrang Roots · Web Design

A homepage design for [Khudrang Roots](https://khudrangroots.org), a student-led initiative of United Sikh Movement that helps students design, fund and lead projects for Panjab's recovery.

The design is built around **seva** (selfless service) and the land and cloth of **Panjab**:

- **Phulkari and dyed cotton.** Madder red, indigo, silk gold and cream, with running-stitch borders and torn-paper edges between sections.
- **Sarson fields.** Mustard flowers grow along the bottom of the hero and lean gently away from the cursor.
- **ਸੇਵਾ as the centrepiece.** The word is set large and filled with a bagh pattern.
- **Gurmukhi alongside English.** The hand-lettered ਖੁਦਰੰਗ ਰੂਟਸ sits above the headline.

All copy comes from the live site.
